# Day 120: Mock Interview and Next Steps

**Teach:** Run a realistic mock: clarify requirements, solve a DSA problem aloud, answer a Java/backend question, then present the capstone.
**Learn:** Self-assessment, interview feedback, targeted revision, and a sustainable job-search routine.
**Build:** Create a 30-day continuation plan based on gaps: DSA, Java/Spring depth, frontend, SQL, system design, or applications.
**Interview:** Explain your capstone architecture and answer follow-ups on security, persistence, testing, and a design tradeoff.
**DSA:** Choose one previously missed problem, solve it again without notes, and record the mistake pattern and corrected reasoning.
**Checkpoint:** Student has a portfolio-ready capstone, a clear explanation of their work, and a specific interview/job-search plan.
