# Day 61: JPA Relationships

**Teach:** Draw the relational relationship first, then map its ownership and lifecycle into entities; avoid adding mappings without a use case.
**Learn:** `ManyToOne`, `OneToMany`, owning side, join columns, and relationship consistency.
**Build:** Model task ownership or library loans with a foreign-key relationship and test persistence.
**Interview:** What is the owning side of a JPA relationship, and why does it matter?
**DSA:** Determine whether a graph is bipartite using BFS coloring.
**Checkpoint:** Student explains how the object relationship maps to a database foreign key.
