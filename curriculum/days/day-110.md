# Day 110: Polyglot Persistence and Boundaries

**Teach:** Draw the consistency boundary between PostgreSQL business records and MongoDB activity documents; discuss failure and duplication risks.
**Learn:** Polyglot persistence, eventual consistency, idempotency, outbox pattern conceptually, and why cross-database writes are hard.
**Build:** Record activity for a task change with a documented failure strategy; do not imply two independent writes are atomic.
**Interview:** How would you keep an activity feed consistent when the primary database and document store are separate?
**DSA:** Solve Design Hit Counter or a time-window event counter; state assumptions and data structure costs.
**Checkpoint:** Student identifies the source of truth and explains what happens if activity recording fails.
