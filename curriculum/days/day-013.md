# Day 13: Constructors and Immutability

**Teach:** Compare partially initialized objects with constructor-validated objects. Discuss when immutable data simplifies reasoning.
**Learn:** Constructor overloads, `this`, `final`, defensive design, and records as a concise data carrier.
**Build:** Model a loan with required member, book, checkout date, and due date; reject impossible combinations.
**Interview:** What makes an object immutable, and what benefits does immutability provide?
**DSA:** Merge two sorted arrays into sorted order; trace pointer movement and state linear complexity.
**Checkpoint:** Student creates only valid loan instances and can name their invariants.
