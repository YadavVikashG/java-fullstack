# Day 27: Streams and Optional

**Teach:** Translate a simple loop into a stream pipeline, compare readability, and identify operations that make streams lazy.
**Learn:** `filter`, `map`, `sorted`, `collect`, terminal operations, and deliberate `Optional` use.
**Build:** Create library reports for available titles and overdue loans; avoid side effects inside stream operations.
**Interview:** What is the difference between `map` and `filter` in a stream?
**DSA:** Merge two sorted lists and remove duplicates; state the sorted-input assumption.
**Checkpoint:** Student can trace a pipeline and explain when a normal loop is clearer.
