# Day 70: Tokens, Cookies, and Sessions

**Teach:** Compare server-side sessions and signed tokens by lifecycle, revocation, storage, and threat model; avoid calling any option universally best.
**Learn:** Session cookies, JWT claims and signatures, expiration, refresh risks, and secure browser storage considerations.
**Build:** Choose and document one authentication approach for the app; implement expiration and logout/revocation behavior appropriate to it.
**Interview:** What does a JWT signature guarantee, and what does it not guarantee?
**DSA:** Solve edit distance for short strings with a DP table and explain its transitions.
**Checkpoint:** Student traces login and authenticated requests and identifies where credentials could be exposed.
