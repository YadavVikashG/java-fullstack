# Day 55: Service Layer and Business Rules

**Teach:** Put a business rule in the service and ask why it does not belong in a controller or repository.
**Learn:** Application service responsibilities, orchestration, domain validation, and thin controllers.
**Build:** Implement checkout and return workflows in a service; controller delegates and translates the result.
**Interview:** What belongs in a service layer, and what is a sign that it has become too large?
**DSA:** Perform a flood fill on a grid using BFS; guard against revisiting cells.
**Checkpoint:** Business rules can be tested without starting the web server.
