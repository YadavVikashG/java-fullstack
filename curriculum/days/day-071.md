# Day 71: CSRF, CORS, and Browser Boundaries

**Teach:** Contrast a browser's same-origin policy, CORS permission, and CSRF risk; do not treat CORS as authentication.
**Learn:** Origins, preflight requests, cookie credentials, CSRF defenses, and safe local development configuration.
**Build:** Configure only the frontend origin needed for development and enable CSRF defenses appropriate to the chosen auth mechanism.
**Interview:** What does CORS control, and why is it not a substitute for authorization?
**DSA:** Solve word break with dynamic programming and test repeated-prefix cases.
**Checkpoint:** Student can explain one rejected cross-origin request and one CSRF mitigation.
