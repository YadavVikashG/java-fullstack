# Day 115: Cloud and Architecture Choices

**Teach:** Start with operational needs and map managed services to responsibilities; avoid choosing microservices just to sound advanced.
**Learn:** Monolith versus services, managed database, object storage, CDN, secrets, network boundaries, and cost awareness.
**Build:** Revise the capstone deployment diagram for a modest real-world workload and estimate its operational components.
**Interview:** When would you keep a modular monolith instead of splitting into microservices?
**DSA:** Solve a greedy interval scheduling problem and explain why the chosen greedy rule works.
**Checkpoint:** Student can justify architecture from requirements and name costs/risks of the chosen services.
