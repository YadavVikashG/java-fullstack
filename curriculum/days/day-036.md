# Day 36: Constraints and Normalization

**Teach:** Use duplicate and inconsistent sample data to motivate constraints and normalization; avoid treating normal forms as ritual.
**Learn:** `NOT NULL`, `UNIQUE`, `CHECK`, foreign keys, and the purpose of first/second/third normal forms.
**Build:** Add uniqueness and validity constraints to the library schema; remove duplicated member details from loans.
**Interview:** What is normalization, and what update anomaly does it help prevent?
**DSA:** Reorder a linked list by alternating first and last nodes; split, reverse, and merge.
**Checkpoint:** Student demonstrates that the database rejects one invalid or duplicate record.
