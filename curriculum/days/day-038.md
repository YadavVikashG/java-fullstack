# Day 38: Aggregation and Grouping

**Teach:** Start with a business question, identify grouping keys, and explain the order of `WHERE`, grouping, and `HAVING` conceptually.
**Learn:** `COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `GROUP BY`, and `HAVING`.
**Build:** Report active loans per member, inventory per category, and monthly expense totals.
**Interview:** What is the difference between `WHERE` and `HAVING`?
**DSA:** Implement a stack that supports retrieving its minimum; discuss the extra state needed.
**Checkpoint:** Student groups at the correct granularity and tests empty or null-containing data.
